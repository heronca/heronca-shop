Our story

Vintage. New. Ours.

Heron CA is a family shop on the Venice Beach boardwalk. We bring together curated vintage, new with tags finds and our own Coastal Apparel, designed in California.

Every piece is cleaned, checked and picked by hand before it reaches the rack. Most are one of one, so when it's gone, it's gone.
