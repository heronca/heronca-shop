Who we are

A shop built on not walking past things

Heron started as a habit. We could not leave a good piece behind, so we stopped pretending and started doing it on purpose.

We look through Europe, America and Asia for brands that do not usually make it to this coast, and we bring back one of each. No racks of the same shirt in five sizes. What is here is what there is.

We are a family business. Our family has run Central Tailor and Alterations in Mountain View for years, and Heron is the next chapter, here on the boardwalk.

What you'll find

Three kinds of thing, and nothing filler

New with tags

Never worn, still labelled. Brands that normally mean a plane ticket or a shipping form, sitting on a rack in Venice.

Past seasons

Single pieces that did not come back. The size is the size, and that is usually how the good ones stay affordable.

Objects and art

The things we could not leave behind. Odd finds, small runs, pieces that belong on a shelf instead of a hanger.

How it gets here

Everything is gone over before it goes out

Washed if it can be, aired if it can't. Nothing torn, nothing stained. If something has a mark on it, we say so before you ask. That is the whole promise, and it is the reason we are comfortable putting our name on pieces we did not make.

Returns

All sales are final

Most of what we sell is one of one, so we can't take pieces back because they didn't fit or you changed your mind. Please ask for measurements and extra photos before you buy. We're happy to help.

If something arrives that is not what we described, or with damage we didn't mention, like a tear, a stain or a broken zipper, tell us within 7 days of delivery. Email info@heronca.com with your order details and photos. We'll look at every case, and if we approve it, we'll make it right with store credit for the full price of the piece, or another fix we agree on together.

Orders placed on eBay, Poshmark, Vinted and other marketplaces follow that marketplace's own return rules.
